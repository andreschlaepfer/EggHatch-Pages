# EggHatch — Support

A calm, beautifully crafted iPhone app where you hatch eggs, waiting or tapping.

## How to play

- **Tap the egg.** Each tap is a small reward. Cracks gradually appear as you go.
- **Or use a nest.** Tap the clock icon (top-left), pick a duration (15 to 120 minutes), and stay in the app. When the timer finishes, you'll be awarded taps proportional to the time you focused. A 120-minute nest = one full egg.
- **Hatch and collect.** When the threshold is reached, the egg cracks open and reveals a creature. Tap anywhere to start a new egg.
- **Browse your collection.** Tap the grid icon (top-right) anytime to see every species you've hatched.

## Frequently asked questions

**My collection didn't sync to my other device.**
EggHatch uses Apple's iCloud sync, which can take a moment. Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account, are connected to the internet, and have iCloud Drive enabled in iOS Settings. If a few minutes pass and it still hasn't synced, opening the app on each device usually nudges iCloud to refresh.

**I left the app during a nest session and lost my reward.**
That's by design — a nest only rewards you if you stay in EggHatch for the full session. If you switch to another app or force-quit during a session, the egg "gets cold" and the session ends without a reward. **Locking your phone is fine** — you can lock the screen and the session continues normally.

**The app didn't make a sound when I tapped.**
Sound effects respect your phone's silent switch. If your phone is muted, EggHatch is silent. Toggle the silent switch (or check Settings → Sounds & Haptics) to enable sound.

**Can I get more eggs / new creatures?**
Yes — new creatures and eggs roll out as content updates. Just keep the app updated through the App Store.

**There's no shop, no currency, no in-app purchases?**
That's right. EggHatch is a one-time download with no monetization, no subscriptions, and no premium tier. It's just an app.
