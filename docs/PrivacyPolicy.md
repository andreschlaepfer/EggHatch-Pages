# EggHatch — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 3, 2026_

EggHatch is built to be a calm, gentle app. That extends to your data: we don't collect anything personal, and there's no analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs of any kind. This page is short on purpose.

## What EggHatch stores

EggHatch stores a small amount of data **on your device and in your iCloud account**:

- The number of taps on your current egg, so progress survives between sessions.
- The active egg's id, so you don't lose your egg when you close the app.
- A list of creatures you've hatched (species id, count, first-hatched date), so your collection persists.
- Your nest-session settings (last duration, notifications on/off).

This data is stored locally via `@AppStorage` and synced via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store **only between your own devices**. It never leaves your iCloud account. We — the developer — never see it, receive it, or have any way to access it.

## What EggHatch does NOT do

- ❌ No analytics or tracking of any kind.
- ❌ No advertising. No ad networks. No ad SDKs.
- ❌ No accounts, no logins, no email collection.
- ❌ No third-party SDKs.
- ❌ No data sold or shared with anyone.
- ❌ No remote servers — everything runs on your device.

## Notifications

If you enable nest-session notifications, EggHatch schedules a **local** notification on your device for the moment your session completes. This notification is generated and delivered entirely on your device — no remote push servers are involved, and no notification provider receives any data about you. You can disable notifications anytime in iOS Settings.

## Children

EggHatch is suitable for users of all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect no personal information from children either.
